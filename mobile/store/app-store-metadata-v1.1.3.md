# App Store & Play Store — Listing Metadata (v1.1.3 repositioning)

**App:** EdLight Academy · **Bundle ID:** `com.edlightacademy`
**Supersedes:** `app-store-metadata.md` (v1.1.0, Bac-forward positioning)

## Why this rewrite
The v1.1.0 copy positioned EdLight as a **Bac exam-prep app** (subtitle "Cours,
quiz et examens", promo "Réussissez le Bac…", keywords led by `bac,examen`). The
product has since matured into a **personalized learning companion for every
Haitian student, 7ᵉ through university** — it adapts the home, practice, review,
and AI tutor to each student's grade, track, and performance. This copy leads with
that maturity; exam prep is now one pillar, not the headline.

**Honesty guardrail:** every claim below reflects what users experience **today** —
a grade-adaptive experience (7ᵉ→post-Bac), spaced-repetition review ("à réviser"),
difficulty-ordered content, a personalized study plan, and an AI tutor that adapts
to the student's level. The crowd-calibrated difficulty engine (auto-activates
~Oct 2026, per-question gated) is deliberately **not** headlined as live.

Character limits unchanged. Apple: name ≤30, subtitle ≤30, promo ≤170, keywords
≤100, description ≤4000. Google Play: title ≤30, short description ≤80, full
description ≤4000.
